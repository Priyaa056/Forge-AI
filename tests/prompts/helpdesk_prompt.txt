Build an IT Help Desk application called "FixIt Desk" for a mid-size company.

1. Ticket Management
   - Employees submit tickets with title, description, category, and priority.
   - Track open, in progress, resolved, and closed tickets.
   - Show overdue tickets based on the due date.
2. Asset Management
   - Register IT assets with asset tag, type, serial number, purchase date, and assigned employee.
3. Knowledge Base
   - Publish articles with title, content, and tags.

Roles:
- Admin: Full system access.
- Technician: Ticket management and asset management.
- Employee: Submit tickets and view articles.

Dashboard: display open tickets, overdue tickets, tickets by priority, and recent activity.
Use an indigo accent color.
