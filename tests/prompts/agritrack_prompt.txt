Build a complete full-stack web application called AgriTrack – Smart Farm Management System.
The application should help farmers and agricultural businesses manage farms, crops, field activities, workers, equipment, irrigation, harvests, and sales.
1. UI and Design
Create a modern, professional agricultural SaaS dashboard.

* Use an earthy green, white, and light beige color palette.
* Create a collapsible sidebar and top navigation bar.
* Use clean cards, charts, tables, icons, and responsive layouts.
* Provide desktop, tablet, and mobile support.
* Use relevant agricultural icons and visual indicators.

2. Dashboard
Display:

* Total farms.
* Total cultivated land.
* Active crops.
* Workers currently employed.
* Equipment in use.
* Upcoming harvests.
* Monthly expenses.
* Total sales revenue.

Include:

* Crop production chart.
* Monthly revenue and expense chart.
* Farm-wise crop distribution.
* Upcoming farm activities.
* Recent alerts and notifications.

3. Farm Management
Allow users to:

* Register farms.
* Enter farm name, location, area, soil type, and ownership details.
* Divide farms into individual fields.
* View field boundaries and cultivation status.
* Track farm activities and production history.

4. Crop Management
Manage:

* Crop name.
* Crop variety.
* Planting date.
* Expected harvest date.
* Field assignment.
* Seed quantity.
* Growth stage.
* Crop health status.
* Expected production.

Provide crop lifecycle tracking from planting to harvesting.
5. Irrigation Management

* Register irrigation systems.
* Track water usage.
* Schedule irrigation activities.
* Record irrigation history.
* Monitor water availability.
* Generate water consumption reports.

6. Worker Management

* Register farm workers.
* Maintain employee profiles.
* Assign workers to farms and activities.
* Track attendance.
* Manage work schedules.
* Calculate wages and payments.

7. Equipment Management
Manage tractors, harvesters, pumps, and other agricultural equipment.
Include:

* Equipment name.
* Equipment category.
* Registration number.
* Purchase date.
* Maintenance history.
* Operating status.
* Next service date.
* Maintenance expenses.

8. Fertilizer and Pesticide Management

* Maintain fertilizer and pesticide records.
* Track product quantities.
* Record application dates.
* Assign applications to specific fields and crops.
* Track stock levels and expiry dates.
* Generate usage reports.

9. Harvest Management

* Record harvested crops.
* Select farm and field.
* Enter harvest date.
* Record harvested quantity.
* Track quality grade.
* Record storage location.
* Maintain harvest history.

10. Sales and Orders

* Register buyers.
* Create crop sales orders.
* Track crop quantities and prices.
* Record delivery information.
* Manage payment status.
* Generate sales invoices.

11. Financial Management
Track:

* Seed expenses.
* Fertilizer expenses.
* Worker wages.
* Equipment maintenance costs.
* Irrigation expenses.
* Transportation expenses.
* Crop sales revenue.
* Profit and loss.

Generate monthly and yearly financial reports.
12. Reports and Analytics
Create reports for:

* Crop production.
* Farm performance.
* Worker attendance.
* Equipment utilization.
* Water consumption.
* Fertilizer usage.
* Harvest quantities.
* Sales and expenses.

Include date filters and CSV export.
13. User Roles
Implement:

* Admin: Full application access.
* Farm Manager: Manage farms, crops, workers, and activities.
* Field Supervisor: Update field activities and crop progress.
* Accountant: Manage expenses, payments, and sales.
* Worker: View assigned activities and work schedules.

Enforce permissions through backend APIs.
14. Technical Requirements
Frontend:

* React.
* TypeScript.
* Vite.
* Tailwind CSS.
* React Router.
* Recharts.
* Lucide React.

Backend:

* Python FastAPI.
* SQLAlchemy.
* Pydantic.
* REST APIs.

Database:

* PostgreSQL, with SQLite support for local testing if appropriate.

Implement authentication, validation, database relationships, error handling, and CRUD operations.
15. Functional Requirements

* All sidebar navigation must work.
* All forms must save data through backend APIs.
* Dashboard statistics must reflect actual database records.
* Search, filtering, and sorting must work.
* Related records must be connected correctly.
* Include realistic synthetic demo data.
* Avoid nonfunctional buttons and placeholder pages.
* Include loading, success, error, and empty states.
* Start the generated application locally and verify its preview.

Final Requirement
Generate a complete working Smart Agriculture Management System.
Do not reuse unrelated modules or default application structures from previous projects. Derive the navigation, database entities, pages, workflows, and dashboard from this application's actual requirements.
