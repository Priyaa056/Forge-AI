Build a complete Hospital Management System for a multi-specialty hospital named "MediCare Hospital".

The application should have a modern, professional healthcare dashboard with a clean blue-and-white theme, responsive layout, sidebar navigation, top navigation bar, statistics cards, charts, tables, and interactive forms.

The system should support the following modules:

1. **Dashboard**
   - Display total patients, doctors, appointments, available beds, admitted patients, and monthly revenue.
   - Show appointment trends, patient admission charts, department statistics, and recent activities.

2. **Patient Management**
   - Register new patients with name, age, gender, contact number, address, blood group, and medical history.
   - Display all patients in a searchable and filterable table.
   - Allow users to view, edit, and update patient records.

3. **Doctor Management**
   - Add doctors with their name, specialization, department, contact details, and availability.
   - Display doctor profiles and schedules.
   - Allow administrators to update doctor information.

4. **Appointment Management**
   - Book appointments by selecting a patient, doctor, department, date, and time.
   - Display upcoming, completed, and cancelled appointments.
   - Prevent duplicate bookings for the same doctor and time slot.

5. **Department Management**
   - Include Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Dermatology, and General Medicine.
   - Display department information and assigned doctors.

6. **Admission and Bed Management**
   - Admit patients and assign rooms and beds.
   - Track available, occupied, and maintenance beds.
   - Record admission date, assigned doctor, and discharge date.
   - Update bed availability after patient discharge.

7. **Billing and Payments**
   - Generate patient invoices for consultations, treatments, medicines, and room charges.
   - Display total amount, paid amount, and pending balance.
   - Support payment status tracking and printable invoices.

8. **Pharmacy Management**
   - Maintain medicine inventory with medicine name, category, quantity, price, and expiry date.
   - Track low-stock medicines and expired products.
   - Record medicine sales and stock updates.

9. **Staff Management**
   - Manage nurses, receptionists, laboratory technicians, and administrative staff.
   - Store employee details, department, designation, and contact information.

10. **Reports**
   - Generate reports for patient registrations, appointments, admissions, bed occupancy, revenue, and pharmacy inventory.
   - Provide date filters and charts.

## User Roles

Implement role-based access for:
- Admin: Full system access.
- Doctor: Access to assigned patients, appointments, and medical records.
- Receptionist: Patient registration and appointment management.
- Nurse: Patient admission and assigned patient information.
- Pharmacist: Medicine inventory and pharmacy management.

## Technical Requirements

- Frontend: React.js with TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
- Backend: Python FastAPI.
- Database: PostgreSQL.
- Authentication: JWT-based login and role-based authorization.
- API: RESTful endpoints with proper validation and error handling.
- Use reusable React components and a clean folder structure.
- Create proper database relationships between patients, doctors, appointments, departments, admissions, beds, invoices, and medicines.
- Use realistic sample data for initial testing.
- Ensure forms, buttons, filters, navigation, and CRUD operations work correctly.
- Do not use hardcoded dashboard statistics as a substitute for actual application data.
- Include loading states, validation messages, and error handling.
