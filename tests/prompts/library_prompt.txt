Build a Library Management System named "BookNest" for a community library.

Modules:
1. Book Catalog
   - Add books with title, author, ISBN, genre, publication year, and number of copies.
   - Track available, borrowed, and lost books.
2. Member Management
   - Register members with name, email, phone, and membership type.
3. Loans
   - Issue loans by selecting a member, book, loan date, and due date.
   - Prevent duplicate loans for the same member and book.
   - Show overdue loans.
4. Fines
   - Record fines with amount, paid amount, and reason.
5. Reports
   - Monthly loan reports with date filters.

User Roles:
- Admin: Full system access.
- Librarian: Book catalog management and loan management.
- Member: View books.

Dashboard: display total books, borrowed books, overdue loans, and recent activity.
Use a dark green sidebar and include realistic sample data.
